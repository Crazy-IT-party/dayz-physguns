A DayZ mod with two guns: a **gravity gun** in the spirit of Half-Life 2 that grabs, carries, throws and punts
anything loose, and a **portal gun** in the spirit of Portal that links two portals anything can pass through,
keeping its speed. Both are held like a stockless AK, run on six 9V batteries and are made for admins to hand out:
they are not in the loot economy.

Client + server mod `@PhysGuns`. Its own models, textures, materials and sounds, all generated by the scripts in
`tools/` - no assets from anywhere else.

## The gravity gun (`PG_GravityGun`)

- **Punt:** tap fire - whatever is in front of it (up to 7 m) is knocked away: items, infected, animals, players,
  even vehicles (a car only gets a nudge).
- **Grab and carry:** hold fire - it catches what it points at, pulling it in from up to 12 m, and holds it in front
  of you in a crackling cage while the trigger stays down; it follows where you look. Up to 350 kg.
- **Throw:** press reload (or tap fire / the secondary button) while carrying.
- **Drop:** let go of fire.
- **Creatures:** infected, animals and players can be lifted and thrown; one thrown hard into a wall or the ground
  is hurt like a fall from that speed - a player is knocked out and can break legs.
- **Effects:** a beam from its claws, a glow, a hum that rises as you swing what it holds, and its own grab, drop,
  punt, throw and dry-fire sounds.

## The portal gun (`PG_PortalGun`)

- **Two portals, blue and orange,** on any fixed surface - ground, walls, rocks, buildings - up to 250 m away; not on
  things that move. Each gun has its own pair; a new portal of a colour replaces the old one.
- **Fire** opens the portal of the selected colour; the **fire mode** button (or **B**) switches blue / orange, and the
  lit lamp on top of the gun shows which. The secondary button (middle mouse, or holding breath while aiming) always
  opens orange.
- **Reload** closes both portals.
- **Aiming** (gun raised) shows a faint outline where the portal will open.
- **Going through:** players, infected, animals and items that go into one come out of the other, turned the way it
  faces, with the speed they went in at - fall into a floor portal and you fly out of a wall one.
- **Sound:** a shot, a "whomp" as a portal opens, and a low static hum around each open portal.
- A portal is a glowing oval: the game can't render the view through it.

## Batteries

Each gun has six `9V Battery` slots and needs all six charged; with any missing or flat it only clicks and says so.
A full set (6 × 50) lasts about 300 punts, 200 throws, 20 minutes of carrying or 150 portals:

| action | uses |
|---|---|
| punt | 1 |
| throw | 1.5 |
| carrying / pulling | 0.25 a second (0.5 needed to start a grab) |
| a portal | 2 (only when it opens) |

A gun spawned with the game's debug spawn - Community Online Tools' object spawner in its debug-spawn mode, for one -
comes with six fresh batteries; one spawned any other way comes empty.

## Condition

Both guns wear like any weapon - pristine, worn, damaged, badly damaged, ruined - with their own damage textures:
scratches, rust and soot, the lamps dimming and going out.

## Controls

| | keyboard + mouse | gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| punt / portal | fire | fire |
| grab and carry | hold fire | hold fire |
| throw (gravity) / close portals | reload | reload |
| blue / orange (portal) | B or fire mode | fire mode |
| secondary: throw / orange | middle mouse | hold breath while aiming |

The middle mouse and B bindings are in the game's controls under **PhysGuns** and can be changed.

## Contents

- `mod/PhysGuns` - the addon: config, scripts, models (MLOD `.p3d`), textures (`.paa`), materials (`.rvmat`),
  sounds (`.ogg`) and the input bindings. Pack it into a pbo with the prefix `PhysGuns` and sign it with your key.
- `mod/mod.cpp` - the mod's name and author, for the `@PhysGuns` folder.
- `tools/` - the generators: `build_guns.py` (the guns' models, textures and damage materials), `build_models.py`
  (portals, beam), `build_sounds.py` (every sound, synthesised), `build_pbo.py` (packs the addon), `paa.py`.

## Server

1. Make the `@PhysGuns` folder - `addons/physguns.pbo` (and its `.bisign`), `mod.cpp`, `keys/` with your `.bikey` -
   put it in the server folder and load it on server and clients (`-mod=...;@PhysGuns`).
2. Spawn the guns as an admin: `PG_GravityGun`, `PG_PortalGun` (and `Battery9V` for spares). They are not in
   `types.xml` and never appear as loot.

## Building

Python 3 with numpy and Pillow, `python-lzo` (for `paa.py`), `ffmpeg` with libvorbis (sounds) and
[armake2](https://github.com/KoffeinFlummi/armake2) (rapifies the materials):

```
python3 -I tools/build_models.py
python3 -I tools/build_guns.py
python3 -I tools/build_sounds.py
python3 -I tools/build_pbo.py mod/PhysGuns @PhysGuns/addons/physguns.pbo
```

## Known limits

- Dead bodies are left alone: once a body has settled, the server can't move it so that clients see it.
- A vehicle can only be punted, and only a little.
- The guns' models have no skeleton, so the game logs a harmless "Weapon has no skeleton" warning.
